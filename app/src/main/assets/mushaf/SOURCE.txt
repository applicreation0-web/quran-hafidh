The Brotli files are losslessly compressed versions of the same SVG pages.
They are decompressed locally in memory when a page is opened.

The quran-svg project states that its KFQC page vectors derive from the
King Fahd Complex digital Mushaf al-Madinah and that the KFQC digital
edition is permitted for digital/software use. Quran Unlock pins a reviewed
commit and checks the Git blob hashes of pages 001, 302, and 604 in addition
to verifying the complete 604-page set.

The app does not require a second Quran application and does not require
network access at runtime to display the embedded Mushaf pages.
